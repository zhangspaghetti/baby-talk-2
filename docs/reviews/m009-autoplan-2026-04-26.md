### Locked Design Actions

1. Preserve Warm Paper on both mobile and admin. Fix usage, not identity.
2. Keep Fraunces restricted to display-level emotional anchors and phrase moments.
3. Make the first screenful answer one question fast: “what do I do next?” on mobile, “what do I need to work on?” on admin.
4. Replace stacked diagnostic noise with calmer localized recovery.
5. Keep deep linking, queue/detail, and explicit stale/error handling as first-class admin strengths.
6. Use `ui-ux-pro-max` only where it strengthens the existing language: paper calmness, dense-but-readable operator rhythm, lazy loading, loading feedback, and recovery affordances.
