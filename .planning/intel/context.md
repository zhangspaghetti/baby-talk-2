## Topic: Baby Talk v1 micro-ritual thesis

- source: `docs/Baby_Talk_Product_Architecture_Spec_vNext.md`
- note: Baby Talk v1 should be treated as a family English micro-ritual system for Chinese 0-3 families, not an infinite phrase generator, course app, translator, or general parenting support system. The first proof is whether a few low-pressure English sounds survive in real routines.

## Topic: Explore vs Activate split

- source: `$gsd-explore` session, 2026-06-14
- note: Parents may freely explore expert content, future routes, and phrase variants, but only a small subset should be activated into daily family routine. This preserves a high-ceiling expert system without turning daily caregiving into a content task.

## Topic: Family English Garden

- source: `$gsd-explore` session, 2026-06-14
- note: Garden is a low-shame memory projection of family micro-ritual transfer. It should show which sounds are candidate, active, familiar, resting, expandable, or belong-to-family, but it should not score parents or make activation decisions.
