An in-process `RitualContentApi` contract and `MockRitualContentApi`
implementation are required Phase 41 scope, not backend creep. Scope drift
begins at deployed network/backend integration, credentials, production service
discovery, AI generation, or production Pack/Graph/Runtime integration.

required. Phase 41 engine behavior must execute reaction, normalized voice
observation, free text, future signal, and strategy preference inputs. The UI
exposes only reaction selection; nonfunctional controls for hidden channels are
forbidden.
