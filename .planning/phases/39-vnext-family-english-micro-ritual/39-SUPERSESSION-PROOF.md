# Phase 39 Supersession Proof

## Canonical Product Contract

Phase 39 locks Baby Talk vNext as a **Family English Micro-ritual** system. This proof is the source-grounded handoff for R058, R059, and R060: future plans must treat a micro-ritual as the product unit, Context Seed as observable evidence, and Joinability as a hypothesis about whether English can lightly join a family moment.

| Contract | Source | Pass/fail rule |
|---|---|---|
| Baby Talk vNext is Family English Micro-ritual first, not a course, translator, check-in, or infinite generation product. | R058, `docs/Baby_Talk_Product_Architecture_Spec_vNext.md`, `39-SPEC.md` | FAIL if a downstream plan optimizes lesson coverage, translation volume, check-in pressure, or generated sentence quantity. |
| Family English Micro-ritual replaces Phrase, Activity, phrase completion, streak, and old GardenGrowth as product truth. | R059, D-11, D-15 | FAIL if Phrase, Activity, phrase completion, streak, or GardenGrowth becomes the vNext unit of progress, success, or family transfer. |
| Context Seed is observed evidence; Joinability is an interpreted hypothesis. | R060, D-18, vNext product spec | FAIL if a plan diagnoses the child, treats hypothesis as fact, or automatically activates content from a context match. |
| Conservative activation and open exploration remain later-phase boundaries. | Phase 40/41 roadmap, R063-R066 | FAIL if Phase 39 work implements activation algorithms, Garden Memory transitions, Runtime Agent payload schemas, or metrics instrumentation. |

The controlling reuse rule is: **素材可以搬运，意义必须重建**. Assets, tone, infrastructure, and interaction references may move only after their product meaning has been rebuilt around Family English Micro-rituals.

Rejected vNext product truths include: course, translator, check-in, infinite generation, phrase completion, streak, and GardenGrowth.
