Chapter 1: Early Communication

Babies begin communicating from birth through crying, cooing, and babbling.
By 6 months, most babies can recognize their name and respond to familiar voices.
Early communication is the foundation for language development.

Chapter 2: First Words

Around 12 months, babies typically say their first words.
Common first words include "mama", "dada", and "no".
Encouraging language through reading and talking helps vocabulary growth.

Chapter 3: Language Milestones

By age 2, most children can combine two words together.
Vocabulary expands rapidly between ages 2 and 3.
Reading aloud daily is one of the most effective ways to support language development.
